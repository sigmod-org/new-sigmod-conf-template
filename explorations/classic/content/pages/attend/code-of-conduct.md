---
title: Code of conduct
---

SIGMOD/PODS 2027 adheres to the [ACM Policy Against Harassment](https://www.acm.org/special-interest-groups/volunteer-resources/officers-manual/policy-against-discrimination-and-harassment).

If you experience unacceptable behavior, you may contact any member of the [SIGMOD Executive Committee](https://sigmod.org/about-sigmod/), the [PODS Executive Committee](https://sigmod.org/pods/pods-organization/), or [DBCares](https://dbdni.github.io/pages/dbcares). Your concerns will be kept in strict confidence, and we will consult with you on any action taken.
