Welcome to the home of the **2028 ACM SIGMOD/PODS Conference**, to be held in Seoul, Korea, on June 17–23, 2028.

**The Conference.** The annual ACM SIGMOD/PODS Conference is a leading international forum for database researchers, practitioners, developers, and users to explore cutting-edge ideas and results, and to exchange techniques, tools, and experiences. The conference includes a fascinating technical program with research and industrial talks, tutorials, demos, and focused workshops. It also hosts a poster session to learn about innovative technology, an industrial exhibition to meet companies and publishers, and a careers-in-industry panel with representatives from leading companies.

**The Venue.** The conference will be held at [COEX](/participants/venue/) in Seoul, Korea. Information on the call for papers, registration, accommodation, and travel will be posted on this website as it becomes available.

## SIGMOD/PODS 2028 Anti-Harassment Policy

SIGMOD/PODS 2028 adheres to the [ACM Policy Against Harassment](https://www.acm.org/special-interest-groups/volunteer-resources/officers-manual/policy-against-discrimination-and-harassment). Any participant who experiences unacceptable behavior may contact any current member of the [SIGMOD Executive Committee](https://sigmod.org/about-sigmod/) and the [PODS Executive Committee](https://sigmod.org/pods/pods-organization/) or [DBCares](http://www.sigmod2020.org/dbcares.shtml). Please be assured that if you approach us, your concerns will be kept in strict confidence, and we will consult with you on any actions taken.

## SIGMOD/PODS 2028 Privacy Policy

SIGMOD/PODS 2028 respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. The conference adheres to the [ACM Privacy Policy](https://www.acm.org/about-acm/privacy-policy). Information collected during registration, participation, and use of conference services will be handled in accordance with ACM's policies and used solely for the purposes of organizing and administering the conference. For full details, please refer to the ACM Privacy Policy.
